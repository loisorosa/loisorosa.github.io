I'm a senior researcher holding a Ramon y Cajal  research contract, and the Scientific director of the Galicia Supercomputing Center (CESGA). My  research is on Computer Architecture, systems and security. My work focuses on memory systems, hardware reliability and security, efficient AI accelerators, microarchitecture, storage systems and processing in memory.

I earned my PhD from University of Santiago de Compostela (USC) under the supervision of [Prof. Elisardo Antelo](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Elisardo_Antelo) and [Prof. Javier Bruguera](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Javier_Bruguera). During my PhD, I visited I-ACOMA research group at University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign, lead by [Prof. Josep Torrellas](http://iacoma.cs.uiuc.edu/josep/torrellas.html). I also adquired industry experience in IBM R&D Labs at Haifa (with [Olga Golovanevsky's](https://www.researchgate.net/scientific-contributions/Olga-Golovanevsky-2028453878), [Marina Biberstein](https://dblp.org/pid/44/5270.html), and [Bilha Mendelson](https://dblp.org/pid/71/2760.html)), and Recore Systems at Enschede (Netherlands). After my PhD, I continued my research on computer architecture with several groups in Industry and Academia, including Universidade Nova de Lisboa with [Prof. Joao Lourenco](https://docentes.fct.unl.pt/joao-lourenco), LSC group at Unicamp with [Prof. Rodolfo Azevedo](https://ic.unicamp.br/en/~rodolfo/), and Xilinx Research Labs at Dublin, with [Michaela Blott](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Michaela_Blott) and [Yaman Umuroglu](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Yaman_Umuroglu). I was a senior researcher during several years at [SAFARI research group](https://safari.ethz.ch) at ETH Zürich, lead by [Prof. Onur Mutlu](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/omutlu). In 2022 I joined CESGA as Director.
