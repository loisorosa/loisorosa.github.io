## Institutional Leadership

**Scientific Director** — [Galicia Supercomputing Center (CESGA)](https://www.cesga.es), Spain (Sep 2025 – present)

Leading CESGA's scientific strategy and serving as institutional representative. PI of the [1HealthAI European AI Factory](https://www.1healthai.eu) (82M EUR), the European AI Factory focused on health and biotechnology.

**Managing Director** — [Galicia Supercomputing Center (CESGA)](https://www.cesga.es), Spain (Mar 2022 – Sep 2025)

Led CESGA's strategic transformation, attracting large-scale European funding and infrastructure investments:
- Galicia Quantum Technology Hub (2022–2030, 154M EUR)
- Quantum Computer — most qubits in Southern Europe at the time (2023, 32 qubits, 14M EUR)
- Finisterrae III — second largest supercomputer in Spain (2022, 7M EUR)
